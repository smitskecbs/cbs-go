CBS GO Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

CBS GO is a location-based mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is stored when you use CBS GO.

1. Information We Collect

CBS GO may collect and process the following types of information:

  • Email address, for example when a player requests or recovers a PIN.
  • Location data, used for gameplay features and location-based interactions.
  • Player profile and gameplay data, such as username, progress, score, inventory, and related game data.
  • Technical and device-related information needed to operate, secure, and improve the app.

2. Wallet Functionality

CBS GO may create a wallet locally on the user's device as part of the app's functionality. This wallet is generated and stored locally on the device unless otherwise explicitly stated inside the app. Users are responsible for securing their own device and access credentials.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information only for purposes related to operating and improving CBS GO, including:

  • providing gameplay features,
  • saving and restoring player progress,
  • handling PIN requests or account-related access,
  • supporting wallet-related game functionality,
  • maintaining app security, stability, and performance.

4. Data Storage

CBS GO stores relevant app and player data using Supabase and related infrastructure needed to operate the service. Some information, such as wallet-related data, may also be stored locally on the user's device.

5. Data Sharing

CBS GO does not sell personal data. Data is only used as needed to operate, maintain, and improve the app and its services.

6. Children's Privacy

CBS GO is not directed to children. Because the app may include wallet-related functionality and account-related features, it is intended for a more mature audience and is not specifically designed for use by children.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data used by CBS GO. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and users should also take care to protect their own devices and credentials.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
